Why does the learning rate matter?

Each update multiplies x by (1 − 2η). When 0 < η < 1, the magnitude shrinks toward zero. At η = 1, x oscillates. Above 1, it grows. This behavior belongs to this particular quadratic; real loss landscapes can behave very differently.

What does the result mean?

The objective is the sum of squared distances from points to their assigned centers (SSE). K-means can settle at a local optimum and depends on initialization and k. The generated groups do not establish that a particular number of clusters is correct for real data.

How are parameters counted?

Between adjacent layers of sizes a and b there are a × b weights. Each non-input neuron adds one bias. This explorer counts those parameters only; a functioning model also needs activation functions, a task, data, and a training procedure.
